Website coverage: all 30 named theory sections, 30 ten-question checks and 30 scaffolded responses are scheduled. Practical lessons remain the majority. The complete official outcome set appears below; period rows identify the outcomes directly supported by this unit.

Official Textiles Technology outcomes

OutcomeExact official wording
TT4-ADJ-01in Stage 4 teachers may adjust the Stage 5 outcomes as appropriate to the needs of students in Years 7 and 8
TT5-SAF-01applies design processes and safe work practices to develop textile projects
TT5-COM-01communicates ideas, concepts and processes for textile design and production
TT5-EVL-01investigates and evaluates design inspiration sources and textile products
TT5-IVT-01explains industry practices and innovative technologies used in textile design and production
TT5-CDT-01investigates and applies construction and decorative techniques to create textile products
TT5-MAT-01justifies the selection and use of materials and technologies for textile design and production
TT5-ENV-01analyses relationships between textile design, production and sustainability

What students are learning

Learning focusIn plain English
Safe project workUse design processes and follow teacher-approved studio and machine practices.
CommunicationUse textiles terminology, annotations, images and folio evidence to explain decisions.
Inspiration and evaluationInvestigate inspiration and judge products against functional and aesthetic criteria.
Industry and innovationExplain how industry practice and textile technologies influence design and production.
Construction and decorationInvestigate and apply authorised construction and decorative techniques.
Materials and technologiesTest and justify materials, tools and technologies for the intended end use.
SustainabilityAnalyse environmental relationships across textile design, production, use and care.
Project and folioDesign, produce, document and evaluate source-backed textile projects individually and collaboratively.

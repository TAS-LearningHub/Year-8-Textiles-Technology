Media and visual provenance

Purposeful, privacy-safe assets

Visuals

Course visuals were newly generated and independently inspected at full size. Two original, already-published network assets were reused. No student work or unknown-rights Drive image is published.

Video learning

Public clips are section-matched, current-availability checked and click-to-load through privacy-enhanced embeds. Every section has a complete non-video pathway.

Editable presentations

Each module presentation is built from this course’s theory, approved visuals and public-safe source record. Speaker notes retain a source block.

Deliberate exclusions

Not published or treated as authority

  • past student work, names, photographs, marks and personal-profile material
  • private Drive identifiers, credentials and class-administration records
  • licensed pattern copies, licensed textbook content and unknown-rights mood-board images
  • historical marksheets presented as current assessment authority
  • unverified machine settings, pattern dimensions, construction procedures or local risk controls
  • invented task dates, weighting, outcomes, rubric or submission method
